Chicken Biryani
9
Chicken,500g pieces
Basmati Rice,2 cups, soaked
Yogurt,1/2 cup
Onions,2 , thinly sliced
Tomatoes,2, chopped
Garlic,4 cloves, minced
Ginger,1 inch, grated
Salt,to taste
Ghee,for cooking
7
Heat ghee/oil in a pan, add sliced onions and fry until golden brown. Remove half of the fried onions and set aside for garnish.
Add garlic, ginger, green chilies, and tomatoes to the pan. Cook until tomatoes are soft.
Add chicken pieces, biryani masala, red chili powder, turmeric powder, salt, and yogurt. Cook until chicken is tender.
In a separate pot, bring water to boil. Add soaked rice and cook until 70% done. Drain excess water.
Layer half of the rice in a deep pan, then add the cooked chicken mixture, fried onions, mint leaves, and coriander leaves. Top with the remaining rice.
Cover and cook on low heat for 15-20 minutes until the rice is fully cooked and aromatic.
Serve hot with raita or salad.

Chicken Karahi
14
chicken, 500g, pieces
Tomatoes, 3, chopped
Onions, 2, thinly sliced
Garlic, 4 cloves, minced
Ginger, 1-inch, grated
Green Chilies, 2, chopped
Cumin Seeds, 1 teaspoon 
Coriander Powder, 1 teaspoon 
Turmeric Powder, 1/2 teaspoon 
Red Chili, 1/2 teaspoon 
Garam Masala, 1/2 teaspoon 
Salt, to taste
Coriander Leaves, for garnish
Ghee, for cooking
6
Heat ghee/oil in a karahi or deep pan, add cumin seeds and let them crackle.
Add sliced onions and cook until golden brown.
Add minced garlic, grated ginger, and chopped green chilies. Sauté for a minute.
Add chopped tomatoes, turmeric powder, coriander powder, red chili powder, and salt. Cook until tomatoes are soft.
Add chicken pieces and cook until meat is tender and the masala is well-coated.
Sprinkle garam masala and garnish with fresh coriander leaves before serving.

Green Chilies, 2, Chopped
Ginger, 1-Inch, Grated
Garlic, 4 Cloves, Minced
Cumin Seeds, 1 Teaspoon

Cream, 1/2 Cup

Heat ghee/oil in a pan, add cumin seeds and let them splutter.
Add chopped onions and sauté until golden brown.
Add minced garlic, grated ginger, and chopped green chilies. Sauté for a minute.
Add tomato puree, turmeric powder, and salt. Cook until oil separates.
Add chopped spinach and cook until wilted.

Sprinkle garam masala before serving.
Gajar Ka Halwa
7
Carrots, 1, Kg, Grated
Milk, 1 Liter 
Sugar, 1 Cup 
Ghee, 1/2 Cup (Clarified Butter)
Nuts, (Almonds, Cashews, Pistachios)1/4 Cup Chopped 
Cardamom Powder, 1/4 Teaspoon 
Saffron Strands, (Optional)
5
In a heavy-bottomed pan, heat ghee and add grated carrots. Cook until carrots turn soft.
Add milk and cook on low heat until the milk evaporates and the mixture thickens.
Add sugar, cardamom powder, and saffron strands. Mix well and cook until the sugar dissolves.
Stir in chopped nuts and cook for another 5 minutes.
Garnish with more nuts before serving.

13
Lentils, 1 Cup Washed
Onions, 1, Finely Chopped
Tomatoes, 2, Chopped
Garlic, 4 Cloves, Minced
Ginger, 1-Inch, Grated
Green Chilies, 2, Chopped
Cumin Seeds, 1 Teaspoon 
Turmeric Powder, 1 Teaspoon 
Red Chili, 1/2 Teaspoon 
Salt, To Taste
Coriander Leaves, For Garnish
Rice, Cooked For Serving
Ghee, For Cooking
7
Boil lentils in water until soft and mushy. Set aside.
Heat ghee/oil in a pan, add cumin seeds and let them crackle.
Add chopped onions and cook until golden brown.
Add minced garlic, grated ginger, and chopped green chilies. Sauté for a minute.
Add chopped tomatoes, turmeric powder, red chili powder, and salt. Cook until tomatoes are soft and the mixture thickens.
Add boiled lentils to the tomato mixture and simmer for 5 minutes.
Garnish with fresh coriander leaves and serve hot with cooked rice.
